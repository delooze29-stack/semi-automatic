The storyTwenty years in a notebook

“I wrote these lyrics years ago and never got to hear them. Now I can.”

Semi Automatic started with a stack of lyrics and poems written about twenty years ago, songs that only ever existed on paper.

Many of the songs are built from those original words, kept as close to how they were written as possible, and brought to life with AI using the band's own voice. The words are human. The band is semi automatic.

Hearing those old lyrics finally become songs did something unexpected: it got me writing again. So alongside the songs from the notebook, there are brand-new ones too, written fresh for Semi Automatic, with more on the way.

The sound sits somewhere between rock and folk, in the spirit of Ocean Colour Scene: big guitars, warm melodies and choruses made for singing back.
